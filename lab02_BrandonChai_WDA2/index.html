Subtopic 1

Benefits canned soft drinks have brought

The main benefits come from the can as a package. Select each benefit to see how it changed the storage, transport and use of soft drinks.

Selected benefit

Protection: a strong seal

The sealed aluminium package blocks light and limits contact with air. It helps protect the drink during storage and transport.

Why it matters

A tough package reduces leaks and damage before the drink reaches the user.

Balanced view

Packaging benefits are not health claims

Cans make soft drinks easier to protect, transport and recycle. However, different drinks contain different amounts of sugar, sweeteners or caffeine. The product label helps consumers make an informed choice.

Subtopic 2

Soft-drink regulations in Singapore

Singapore’s rules help consumers understand what they are buying and encourage responsible handling of beverage containers.

Singapore rule board

Look for these signs

A B C D
10¢ Deposit Mark

Nutri-Grade communicates sugar and saturated-fat levels. The Deposit Mark identifies containers that qualify for a refund.

Rule 1 · Nutrition information

Nutri-Grade A to D

Prepacked soft drinks are graded A, B, C or D according to their sugar and saturated-fat content. Grade C and D drinks must show the Nutri-Grade mark on the front of the package.

Remember: Showing the mark is optional for Grade A and B drinks.

Current in July 2026

Return the right can

During the transition period from 1 April to 30 September 2026, only eligible cans carrying the Deposit Mark include the 10-cent deposit. Keep the can empty, uncrushed and with its barcode visible before returning it.
